ROADS loan origination prototype

Weekly release notes

November 2–8, 2026 · Changes recorded November 3–5
The prototype now supports repeatable loan demos, saved application data, rule-driven checklist actions, and Summary figures calculated from loan inputs.

Application data & demo controls

  • Loan-owned persistence. Saved application fields are stored with the loan rather than the tester, supporting reloads and shared demos.
  • New Demo Controls tab. Reset application data or import a simulated Blend application with one or two borrowers. Compact, fixed-height cards use secondary buttons and inline confirmation.
  • Targeted resets. Reset checklist outputs without clearing application inputs. Separate TRID actions clear its application inputs or manually trigger a new Loan Estimate cycle.
  • Working change log. History records changes to saved values, not initial values. Resetting the application clears the log.

Business rules & checklist behavior

  • TRID. The six saved application inputs trigger “TRID day 0.” Loan Estimate delivery satisfies it; a rate-lock update triggers it again. The Loan Estimate checklist follows this shared rule.
  • ECOA. Blend import starts “30 ECOA days remaining.” The chip clears after a decision is rendered and its letter is generated and delivered.
  • Borrower and decision rules. Two-borrower applications show the joint-credit chip; single-borrower applications do not. A saved credit pull makes the Loan Decision task ready.
  • Demo stage navigation. Hidden controls on the first and last tracker chevrons move the stage backward or forward without persisting it.

Borrower & collateral improvements

  • Added a Collateral Owner dropdown populated from the loan’s borrowers, plus saved monthly property-tax and insurance fields.
  • Single-borrower information no longer needs a borrower subtab. Updated the header eyebrow to SLP and capped contact cards at 435 px.

Calculations & visibility

A more realistic loan summary

November 2–8, 2026

Live Summary calculations

  • Collateral value & L/CV. Use estimated values until reported values are saved; combine applicable collateral evaluations. L/CV is total loan amount divided by collateral value.
  • Pricing & payments. Use saved product, rate, supported fee data, and amortization term to calculate supported loan totals and principal-and-interest payments. A rate does not need to be locked for PITI or DTI.
  • PITI & DTI. PITI is monthly principal, interest, taxes, and insurance divided by gross monthly income. DTI also includes supported monthly liabilities. A PITI tooltip shows the numeric breakdown.
  • Rule of XX. Add L/CV percentage points to the amortization term in years, using actual loan data rather than a fixed example.
  • Primary wage earner. Shared income calculations select the highest-income borrower. The PWE chip changes when saved incomes change; the Summary shows that borrower’s simulated credit score after a hard credit pull.

Credit demo & debugging

  • Simulated credit results. Pull Credit populates sample liabilities for each applicable borrower and stores borrower-specific demo scores. Checklist reset clears credit results while retaining application income.
  • Debug modal. Moved unavailable-calculation details out of the Summary into More → Debug. Short, actionable messages identify missing inputs or unsupported rules.
  • Shared logic & checks. Centralized business rules, pricing data, and income calculations reduce conflicting results. Added focused regression tests for persistence, resets, rules, and calculations.

Still intentionally limited

  • APR, cash reserves, and cash to close remain unavailable where required inputs or calculation policies have not been defined.
  • Unsupported ARM payment assumptions, historical-job income, and planned debt-payoff treatment are surfaced rather than guessed. Missing inputs display as unavailable, not misleading zeroes.
  • TRID and ECOA day labels are fixed demo values. Credit data is simulated; this is not a live bureau integration or production compliance engine.
